Why one bottle is rarely enough

A 60 mL bottle lasts about a month at 1 mL twice a day. Hair grows slowly, so most people see less hair fall at 2 to 3 months and visible growth at 4 to 6 months. Buying one bottle at a time costs more and makes it easy to miss days in between, which is the most common reason minoxidil seems not to work. That is why the 3 month pack is the most popular choice.

How to spot a fake or old bottle

  • The seal is intact. Do not accept a bottle with a broken or missing seal.
  • Batch number and expiry are printed on the box and bottle, not handwritten or missing.
  • The strength is clear: 5% or 2% minoxidil, with the volume in mL.
  • The price is believable. A bottle far cheaper than everywhere else is a warning sign.
  • The seller lets you check before paying. With Minoxpak you can open the parcel in front of the rider and check the seal and expiry before you pay.

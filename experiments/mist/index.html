01 What it is

One medicine,plainly stated.

MinoxPak is minoxidil in a clear solution, delivered as a measured spray to the scalp. It is used for androgenetic alopecia: the common, inherited pattern of thinning in men and women.

Minoxidil prolongs the growth phase of the hair follicle, so that hairs which had become short and fine can grow for longer. Exactly how it does this is not fully known, and we would rather say so than pretend.

It is a treatment, not a cure. It does not work for every kind of hair loss, and it does not work for everyone.

02 How to use

A small ritual,twice a day.

  1. Start with a dry scalp.

    Hair and scalp fully dry. Part the hair so that you can see the thinning area.

  2. Spray on the thinning area.

    Lift the cap and press the pump. Aim at the scalp, not at the hair.

  3. Spread with your fingertips.

    Lightly, over the thinning area only. Then wash your hands with soap and water.

  4. Let it dry.

    Leave it to dry fully before you go to bed, wear a cap or style your hair. Do not wash it out for a few hours.

03 What to expect

It works at the root,and at the speed of hair.

The first weeks

Some extra shedding can happen as older hairs make way. It is a normal part of the process and it passes.

The quiet months

Nothing much to see. The work is under the skin, in the follicle. The only task is to keep the routine.

About month four

For most people who respond, this is when results usually begin to show. Not before, and not for everyone.

As long as you use it

The effect lasts only while you keep using it. If you stop, the regrown hair is gradually lost over the following months.

04 Safety

Read this partas carefully as the rest.

Do not use it

  • Anywhere but the scalp. It is for external use only.
  • On red, inflamed, irritated or broken scalp skin.
  • If you are pregnant or breastfeeding.
  • If you are under 18.
  • Near the eyes. If it gets in, rinse well with cool water.

Stop and see a doctor if you notice

  • Chest pain or a fast heartbeat.
  • Dizziness or feeling faint.
  • Sudden, unexplained weight gain.
  • Swelling of the hands or feet.
  • Scalp irritation that does not settle.

Talk to a doctor or pharmacist before you start if you have heart disease or low blood pressure, or if you take medicine for either. Keep the bottle away from children. Keep it, and freshly treated skin, away from pets, especially cats: minoxidil is dangerous to them. Mild itching or dryness of the scalp is the most common unwanted effect.

06 Questions

Short answers,no surprises.

Will it work for me?

It may. Minoxidil helps many people with pattern hair loss, but not all of them, and nobody can tell you in advance. Give it about four months of steady use before you judge.

Is it a cure?

No. It treats pattern hair loss for as long as you use it. It does not help hair loss caused by illness, medicines, diet or scarring.

Why am I shedding more?

In the first weeks some hairs fall earlier than they would have, to be replaced by new growth. If it is heavy or it does not ease, ask a doctor.

Can I use more to speed it up?

No. Using more, or using it more often, does not improve the result. It only makes side effects more likely.

What if I miss a dose?

Carry on with the next one at the usual time. Do not double it to make up.

Which strength is for whom?

The 5% is for men and the 2% is for women. Do not use either if you are pregnant or breastfeeding. If you are unsure, ask a pharmacist.

Minoxidil for beard growth: does it work?

Many men use minoxidil to fill in a patchy beard. There is some evidence it helps, but it is not what minoxidil is licensed for, and facial skin needs more care than the scalp.

Updated 12 November 2026

Important: Minoxpak is labelled for use on the scalp. Using minoxidil on the face is off label. Talk to a doctor or dermatologist before you try it, especially if you have a heart condition or take blood pressure medicine.

What the research says

The main study is a small trial of 48 men in Thailand. Men who applied 3% minoxidil lotion to the beard area twice a day for 16 weeks had a modest but real increase in beard hair compared with men using a placebo (Ingprasert et al., Journal of Dermatology, 2016). There are no large, long-term studies, so nobody knows for sure how well results last after stopping.

What to expect

  • Time: first fine hairs often appear after 2 to 4 months, and they take longer to thicken.
  • Who it helps most: men who already have some beard hair in the patchy areas. It does not create hair follicles that are not there.
  • Stopping: some hairs that have become thick and dark may stay, but finer new hairs often fall out again.

Risks on the face

  • Facial skin is thinner, so redness, dryness and flaking are more common than on the scalp.
  • Keep it well away from the eyes, and wash your hands after use.
  • Do not use more than 1 mL at a time, and never on cut, shaved-raw or irritated skin.
  • Stop and see a doctor if you feel dizzy, get chest pain, a fast heartbeat or swelling.
  • Keep it away from cats, which are very sensitive to minoxidil.

If you and your doctor decide to try it

  1. Try a small patch on your jaw for a few days first to check your skin reacts well.
  2. Apply a small amount to clean, dry skin only where the beard is patchy, and spread it with your fingertips.
  3. Wash your hands, and let it dry fully before touching your face or lying down.
  4. Give it at least 4 months before deciding if it works.

Common questions

Is minoxidil safe for beard growth?

It is not licensed for the face, and facial skin is more sensitive. Speak to a doctor before using it there.

Which strength do people use on the beard?

The study used 3%. Many men use 5% products, which can work but are more likely to irritate facial skin.

Will my beard fall out if I stop?

Some thicker hairs may stay, but finer new hairs often shed. There is not enough research to say for certain.

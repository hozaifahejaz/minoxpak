Directions

  1. Start with a dry scalp. Part your hair so you can see the thinning area.
  2. Apply 1 mL (6 sprays) directly to the scalp, not the hair.
  3. Spread gently with your fingertips, then wash your hands.
  4. Let it dry fully before styling, wearing a cap or going to bed.
  5. Repeat morning and night. Using more does not work faster.

Active ingredient: minoxidil 5% w/v (men) or 2% w/v (women). 60 mL lasts about one month.

Before you use it

  • For adults 18 and over. External use on the scalp only.
  • Do not use if you are pregnant or breastfeeding.
  • Ask a doctor first if you have a heart condition or take blood pressure medicine.
  • Do not apply to a red, irritated, sunburnt or broken scalp.
  • Stop and see a doctor if you get chest pain, a fast heartbeat, dizziness, or swelling of the hands or feet.
  • Keep away from eyes, children and pets.

Questions

How long until I see results?

Most people notice less hair fall in 6 to 8 weeks and visible new growth at 3 to 4 months. Best results show at 6 months or later with twice-daily use.

Is shedding in the first weeks normal?

Yes. Minoxidil pushes out old resting hairs so stronger new ones can grow. It usually settles within 2 months.

Should I choose 5% or 2%?

5% is the standard strength for men. 2% is commonly recommended for women. The quiz will suggest one, and your doctor or pharmacist can advise too.

Why a spray and not a dropper or oil?

A fine mist covers the scalp evenly, measures the dose for you and dries without dripping. Minoxidil is clinically proven to regrow hair. Oils and shampoos are not.

What happens if I stop?

Minoxidil works while you use it. If you stop, hair it helped regrow gradually sheds over the following months.

How do I know it is original?

Is it a subscription?

Are there side effects?

Some people get mild itching, dryness or flaking. Stop use and see a doctor if you feel dizzy or have chest pain or a fast heartbeat. Not for use during pregnancy or breastfeeding.

How it works

Three simple steps, twice a day. Consistency is what grows hair.

Step 1

Part your hair over the thin area

Start with a clean, completely dry scalp. Part your hair so the spray reaches the skin at your crown or hairline.

Step 2

Spray 1 mL directly on the scalp

Six sprays deliver one full dose. Spread gently with your fingertips, then wash your hands.

Step 3

Repeat morning and night

Let it dry for 2 to 4 hours before washing or sleeping on it. Use daily. New growth usually shows in 3 to 4 months.

Why minoxidil works

Minoxidil widens the tiny blood vessels around each hair follicle, bringing more oxygen and nutrients. It pulls resting follicles back into the growth phase and keeps them there longer, so hair grows thicker and stays in longer.

Frequently asked questions

How long until I see results?

Most people notice less hair fall in 6 to 8 weeks and visible new growth at 3 to 4 months. Best results show at 6 months or later with twice-daily use.

Is shedding in the first weeks normal?

Yes. Minoxidil pushes out old resting hairs so stronger new ones can grow. It usually settles within 2 months.

Should I choose 5% or 2%?

5% is the standard strength for men. 2% is commonly recommended for women. If unsure, ask your doctor or pharmacist.

What happens if I stop using it?

Minoxidil works while you use it. If you stop, hair it helped regrow gradually sheds over the following months.

Are there any side effects?

Some people get mild itching, dryness or flaking. Stop use and see a doctor if you feel dizzy or have chest pain or a fast heartbeat. Not for use during pregnancy or breastfeeding.

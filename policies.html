Shipping, returns and privacy

Last updated 12 November 2026. Questions? Message us on WhatsApp at +92 310 3699935.

Shipping

  • We deliver across Pakistan by courier, usually in 2 to 4 working days after your order is confirmed.
  • Delivery is free on orders of Rs. 3,000 or more. Below that, delivery is Rs. 250.
  • Payment is cash on delivery. You pay the courier when the parcel arrives.
  • Every order is confirmed on WhatsApp before it ships. If we cannot reach you, the order is not sent.
  • Parcels are plain, with no product name on the outside.

Returns and refunds

  • If your order arrives damaged, leaking or wrong, message us on WhatsApp within 7 days with a photo. We will send a replacement or refund you, at no cost to you.
  • Unopened bottles can be returned within 7 days of delivery for a refund of the product price. You cover the return courier cost.
  • Opened or used bottles cannot be returned, for hygiene and safety reasons.
  • Refunds are sent by bank transfer or mobile wallet within 7 working days of us receiving the return.
  • If you get a reaction to the product, stop using it and see a doctor. Tell us too, so we can help.

Privacy

  • When you order, your name, mobile number, city and address are sent to us in a WhatsApp message that you send yourself. We use them only to confirm and deliver your order.
  • We share your name, number and address with the courier that delivers your parcel, and with no one else. We do not sell personal details.
  • Your cart is saved in your own browser so it is still there when you come back. It is not sent to us until you order.
  • This site loads fonts from Google Fonts, 3D graphics code from jsDelivr, and photos from Unsplash and Pexels. Those services receive your IP address, as any website you visit does.
  • If you ask for offers on WhatsApp, we message you only on that number, and you can ask us to stop at any time.
  • To see or delete the details we hold about you, message us on WhatsApp.

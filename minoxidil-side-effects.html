Minoxidil side effects: what is normal, and when to stop

Most people use minoxidil on the scalp without trouble. When side effects happen they are usually mild and on the skin. A few signs mean you should stop straight away.

Updated 12 November 2026

Common and usually mild

  • Shedding in the first 2 to 8 weeks. Minoxidil pushes out old resting hairs to make room for new ones. It is a sign the treatment is working and usually settles by month 2 or 3. Do not stop because of it.
  • Itching, dryness or flaking. Often caused by the liquid base rather than the minoxidil itself. Apply to a fully dry scalp, and use a gentle shampoo.
  • Redness or mild irritation. If it does not settle within a week or two, stop and ask a doctor or pharmacist.
  • Unwanted hair on the face. Fine hair on the forehead or cheeks, more common in women. Wash your hands after applying, keep it off your face and pillow, and let it dry before lying down. It usually fades after stopping.

Stop and see a doctor if you get

  • Chest pain, a fast or irregular heartbeat
  • Dizziness or feeling faint
  • Sudden, unexplained weight gain, or swelling of the hands, feet or face
  • A severe rash, blistering or swelling of the scalp

These are rare with minoxidil on the scalp, because only a small amount is absorbed into the body. They are more likely if you use more than 1 mL at a time or apply it to broken or irritated skin.

Who should not use it

  • Under 18s
  • Women who are pregnant or breastfeeding
  • Anyone with a red, sunburnt, infected or broken scalp
  • Ask a doctor first if you have heart disease, or take medicine for blood pressure

Keep it away from cats. Minoxidil is very poisonous to cats, even a small amount licked from skin, hair or a pillowcase. Store it safely and wash your hands after use. If a pet is exposed, see a vet immediately.

How to lower the chance of side effects

  1. Use exactly 1 mL (6 sprays) twice a day, no more.
  2. Apply only to a dry, healthy scalp.
  3. Wash your hands straight after.
  4. Let it dry fully before wearing a cap or going to bed.

Common questions

Is minoxidil shedding permanent?

No. Early shedding is old hair making way for new growth. It usually stops within 2 to 3 months of daily use.

Does minoxidil affect blood pressure?

Minoxidil tablets lower blood pressure. On the scalp only a small amount is absorbed, so this is uncommon, but people with heart conditions or on blood pressure medicine should ask a doctor first.

What happens if I stop using minoxidil?

The hair it helped grow gradually sheds over the following 3 to 4 months, and your hair returns to how it would have been without treatment.
